## 2. Transparency Policy & Ledger Disclosure (`docs/SPONSORSHIP_POLICY.md`)

```markdown
# 16ane Project Funding & Transparency Policy

### Guiding Principles: No Hidden Money
1. **Public Accounting:** All contributions and project disbursements (hosting on Fly.io, domain registration, compute sandboxes) are recorded publicly via Open Collective.
2. **Individual vs. Corporate Sponsorship:**
   - Individuals can sponsor fee-free via GitHub Sponsors.
   - Companies requiring tax invoices and formal fiscal hosting can contribute via Open Collective Europe/US.
3. **No Governance Influence:** Sponsoring does not purchase decision-making control over technical roadmaps, architecture, or license terms.
```
